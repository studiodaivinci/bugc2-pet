# The robot: toy props for the face animations. One spine for the whole batch.
# Painted wooden toys: they rhyme with the golf game's toy box. Shown at about 90 px on a 135x240 screen, so big simple shapes.

# 1 cheetah
A single chunky painted wooden toy cheetah, carved from smooth beech wood in rounded, simplified shapes, painted in glossy enamel: golden yellow body with round black spots, bold black tear stripes running from the inner corners of its eyes down to its mouth, white muzzle and belly, a long tail with black rings and a white tip. It is caught mid-sprint, legs stretched out, a big simple silhouette that reads from across a room. Small glossy black painted eyes with a white highlight. Soft warm studio light from the upper left, gentle shading and one soft glossy highlight. Three-quarter side view, the whole toy in frame and centered, isolated on a transparent background. No text, no base, no ground shadow.
