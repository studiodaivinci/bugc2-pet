# chameleon
A single chunky painted wooden toy chameleon, carved from smooth beech wood in rounded, simplified shapes, painted in glossy enamel: a body that blends in bands from lime green to turquoise to orange, a curly spiral tail, a little crest on its head, and big round turret eyes, each pointing a different way. Standing on a short branch. A big simple silhouette that reads from across a room. Soft warm studio light from the upper left, gentle shading and one soft glossy highlight. Side view, the whole toy in frame and centered, isolated on a transparent background. No text, no ground shadow.
